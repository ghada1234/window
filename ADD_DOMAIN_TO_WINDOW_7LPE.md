## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### "Domain already assigned to another project"

**Solution:**
- When adding domain, Vercel will offer to transfer it
- Click "Transfer" or "Yes"
- Domain moves to window-7lpe automatically

### Domain not loading after 1 hour

**Check:**
1. DNS propagation: https://dnschecker.org
2. Vercel dashboard shows "Active"
3. Try clearing browser cache

**Wait up to 24 hours** for full global propagation (usually much faster)

### SSL certificate not working

**Solution:**
- Vercel generates certificate automatically
- Usually ready in 5-10 minutes
- If not working after 1 hour, contact Vercel support
