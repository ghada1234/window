# 💬 WhatsApp Share Guide - Wellness Report

## 🎉 Share Your Wellness Journey on WhatsApp!

Your Wellness Report can be instantly shared with friends, family, or your wellness coach via WhatsApp with just one click!

---

## 🚀 **How to Share on WhatsApp**

### **Step 1: Generate Your Report**
1. Open the app
2. Navigate to **Sidebar → "Wellness Report"**
3. Your report automatically generates
4. Choose time period (7 days / 30 days / 1 year)

### **Step 2: Click Share Button**
1. Find the green **"Share on WhatsApp"** button
2. Click it
3. Wait 2-3 seconds for PDF generation

### **Step 3: Choose How to Share**

#### **On Mobile Devices (Best Experience):**
✅ WhatsApp app opens automatically  
✅ PDF is attached to the message  
✅ Pre-written message included  
✅ Choose contact or group  
✅ Send!  

#### **On Desktop/Computer:**
✅ PDF downloads to your computer  
✅ WhatsApp Web opens with message  
✅ Manually attach the downloaded PDF  
✅ Choose contact  
✅ Send!

## 🔧 **Troubleshooting:**

### **Problem: WhatsApp doesn't open**
**Solutions:**
1. Check WhatsApp is installed
2. Try opening WhatsApp manually
3. Use the downloaded PDF instead
4. Share via other apps (email, etc.)

---

### **Problem: PDF not attached (Mobile)**
**Solutions:**
1. Check phone permissions
2. Allow file access
3. Try manual download then share
4. Update WhatsApp app

---

### **Problem: File too large**
**Solutions:**
1. Choose shorter time period
2. PDF is typically only 200-500KB
3. Check phone storage
4. Clear WhatsApp cache

---

### **Problem: Message text doesn't appear**
**Solutions:**
1. Copy text manually from preview
2. Type custom message
3. PDF still contains all data
4. Message is optional
