# 📊 Wellness Report - PDF Export & WhatsApp Sharing Guide

## ✅ What's Been Added

### **Wellness Report Feature** 🎉
A comprehensive wellness analytics dashboard that:
- ✅ Calculates your overall wellness score (0-100)
- ✅ Analyzes 5 key health metrics
- ✅ Provides personalized insights and recommendations
- ✅ Tracks achievements and progress
- ✅ **Exports to PDF** 📄
- ✅ **Shares via WhatsApp** 💬

## 🌟 Features Overview

### 1. **Overall Wellness Score**
Visual circular progress showing your total wellness score based on:
- 🍎 **Nutrition** (diet quality and calorie balance)
- 💧 **Hydration** (water intake)
- 🏃 **Activity** (exercise and movement)
- 😴 **Sleep** (sleep duration and quality)
- 😊 **Mood** (emotional well-being)

### 2. **Key Insights**
- **Strengths:** Areas where you're excelling (85%+)
- **Improvements:** Areas needing attention (<70%)
- **Trends:** Overall health patterns
- **Correlations:** How different metrics affect each other

### 3. **Achievements**
Track your wellness milestones:
- 🏆 Wellness Warrior (7 days tracking)
- 💧 Hydration Hero (water goals met)
- 🏃 Active Lifestyle (exercise consistency)
- 😴 Sleep Champion (quality sleep)
- 🎯 Goal Getter (goals completed)

### 4. **Personalized Recommendations**
Priority-based action items:
- **High Priority** - Critical areas needing immediate attention
- **Medium Priority** - Important improvements
- **Low Priority** - Nice-to-have enhancements

## 📄 PDF Export Feature

### **How It Works:**
1. Click **"Download PDF"** button
2. System captures your entire report
3. Converts to high-quality PDF (A4 format)
4. Automatically downloads to your device
5. Success message confirms download

### **PDF Contents:**
✅ Overall wellness score with visual chart  
✅ All 5 health metric scores  
✅ Key insights and analysis  
✅ Achievements earned  
✅ Personalized recommendations  
✅ Generation date and period  

### **PDF Features:**
- **High Quality:** 2x resolution for crisp text and graphics
- **Multi-page:** Automatically splits long reports across pages
- **Professional:** Clean A4 format suitable for printing
- **Timestamped:** Includes generation date in filename

### **Filename Format:**
```
Wellness-Report-MM-DD-YYYY.pdf
Example: Wellness-Report-11-01-2025.pdf
```

## 📊 Scoring System

### **How Scores Are Calculated:**

#### **Nutrition Score (0-100):**
- **95:** Within 200 calories of target (2000)
- **85:** Within 400 calories
- **75:** Within 600 calories
- **60:** Over 600 calories difference

#### **Hydration Score (0-100):**
- **95:** Met or exceeded 2000ml daily goal
- **85:** 80%+ of goal (1600ml+)
- **70:** 60%+ of goal (1200ml+)
- **55:** Below 60% of goal

#### **Activity Score (0-100):**
- **95:** 30+ minutes daily average
- **80:** 20-29 minutes daily
- **65:** 10-19 minutes daily
- **50:** Less than 10 minutes

#### **Sleep Score (0-100):**
- **95:** 7-9 hours (optimal)
- **80:** 6-10 hours (good)
- **65:** 5-11 hours (acceptable)
- **50:** Less than 5 or more than 11 hours

#### **Mood Score (0-100):**
- **100:** Great mood consistently
- **80:** Good mood mostly
- **60:** Okay/neutral mood
- **40:** Bad mood frequently
- **20:** Terrible mood

### **Overall Score:**
Average of all 5 scores = Overall Wellness Score

### **Score Labels:**
- **85-100:** Excellent ✅
- **70-84:** Good 👍
- **50-69:** Fair ⚠️
- **0-49:** Needs Improvement 🔴
