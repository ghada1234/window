## ⚡ Quick 3-Step Setup (Takes 2 Minutes)

---

## Step 1: Open Domain Settings

**Click this link:**

👉 **https://vercel.com/ghada-rabees-projects/window/settings/domains**

This opens the domain settings for your "window" project.

---

## Step 2: Add Your Domain

1. **Click** the big blue **"Add Domain"** button

2. **Type:** `find-your-inner-peace.com`

3. **Click:** "Add"

4. **You'll see a message:** "This domain is assigned to another project"

5. **Vercel will show options:**
   - "Transfer domain to this project" 
   - OR "Remove from [old-project] and add here"

6. **Click:** "Transfer" or "Yes, move it"

7. **Confirm** the transfer

---

## Step 3: Wait for DNS (5-60 minutes)

Vercel automatically:
- ✅ Transfers the domain
- ✅ Generates SSL certificate (HTTPS)
- ✅ Configures DNS
- ✅ Makes it live

**While waiting, you can also add www:**

1. Click "Add Domain" again
2. Type: `www.find-your-inner-peace.com`
3. Add (this will redirect to main domain)

## 🆘 If You Get Stuck

### Common Issues:

**"Domain already assigned"**
- Solution: Click "Transfer" when prompted
- Or: Contact Vercel support (very fast response)

**Domain not working after 1 hour**
- Check: https://dnschecker.org
- Wait: Up to 24 hours max (usually 5-60 min)
- Verify: Vercel dashboard shows domain as "Active"

**Need help?**
- Vercel Support: https://vercel.com/support
- Live chat in dashboard
- Usually < 1 hour response
